Haiku's game tree: how it plays, and what it says about its opponent

Claude Haiku 5.5 played an 8-round Prisoner's Dilemma (both cooperate: 7 each; both defect: 3 each; defecting on a cooperator: 9 vs 1) against 148 opponent strategies written by LLMs, once for each goal it was given. Each dot is a point in the game. Click a dot to open the rounds after it and to see what Haiku does next and what it said about the other player there. A second experiment (in progress) puts Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Fable in the same game and tells them the 148 strategies the opponent is drawn from, so their moves can be compared with the best possible play.
