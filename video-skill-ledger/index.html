Video skill ledger

The skill ledger built while labelling video-benchmark text items, and example text items with their skill trees. A text item is a question stem plus its option set. A labeller (Claude Sonnet) first wrote a short plan of the steps needed to answer it, then looked each step up in the ledger: a two-level tree of skills (L2) and narrower skills (L3) that grew during the run. One text item's skill tree is the set of L2 › L3 paths its steps were labelled with.
