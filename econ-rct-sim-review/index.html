Supervisor review

F1Checks pass. Test suite 112/112 OK (156 s). Tree clean apart from untracked literature/, logs/. I recomputed the per-state MPCs from the raw rows for this page and they match robust_mpc_gptoss_vs_fable.json (medians: gpt-oss consumption 0.000, save 0.495, menu 0.077; Fable 0.143 / 0.465 / 0.135).
F2Fable's reasoning traces were not stored. claude_batch_probe.py keeps only the text block (ac.text_of), so thinking is dropped. At effort low Fable thought on just 46 of 3,000 calls (18,689 thinking tokens), and those are lost. What this page shows for Fable is its one-sentence note field, which is a self-report and not a trace. gpt-oss's full analysis channel is stored for all 3,000 calls. A like-for-like comparison of traces needs a small Fable rerun with thinking kept (and probably a higher effort level).
F3Fable reads the closure "other income" as a windfall. In pure-control states, 45 of 500 Fable notes mention a "transfer" (gpt-oss: 2 of 500), even though neither the persona nor the state mentions one. Example S010: "other income 22106" in one line, so Fable writes "A large transfer arrived…". Other income is the D55 budget-closure residual: median 6,329 KES in pure control, with 25 of 100 sampled states above 10,000. Check whether the rendering invites this reading, because it inflates "menu" purchases in the control group.
F4gpt-oss's response to extra cash is mostly saving. Median consumption MPC is 0.00 (food 0.00); it saves about 0.5 and spends about 0.08 on menu items. Its base plans are infeasible in 33% of cases, against 13% for Fable. The plain means (0.43) are driven by a few absurd plans (one buys 5.87M KES of menu items). This is consistent with the full run's missing consumption effect. Use the sort "|MPC diff|" in States to read the disagreements.
F5Scope. This is a static, single-month probe drawn from one gpt-oss source run, so the states are gpt-oss's own trajectories. It compares the models only and does not show which one is closer to H&S (the builder says the same). Real households' MPC is about 0.76 (Egger). Both models are far below that, and Fable is less far.
F6Minor. The gpt-oss probe's meta.json has flags.logprobs = -1 while logprobs: true and the rows do hold top-5 logprobs. It is a metadata inconsistency only. Fable has no logprobs (unsupported by the API), which is recorded.
